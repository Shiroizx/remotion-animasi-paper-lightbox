# ElevenLabs v4 Script & Voice Guide (Paper-cut Lightbox)

## Official Eleven v4 Expressive Audio Tags
Insert these square-bracket tags `[...]` immediately before any sentence or phrase requiring an emotional shift:
- `[curious]` — questioning, intriguing tone (ideal for the opening Hook).
- `[warm]` / `[gentle]` — intimate, unhurried delivery like a storyteller at a lit desk.
- `[serious]` / `[dramatic]` — grounded, poetic authority when scenes shift scale or stakes rise.
- `[tense]` / `[ominous]` — quiet dread or suspense as lights dim or shadows close in.
- `[sighs]` / `[somber]` — quiet exhale, longing, memory, or loss.
- `[whispers]` — soft, close whisper for secrets behind a window or right before a lamp-off silence beat.
- `[confident]` — calm, decisive cadence for closing lines or cliffhangers.

## Script Writing Rules
1. **Ideal Length:** 95–125 words (~45–55 seconds of voiceover at an unhurried storytelling pace, leaving room for a warm lamp switch-on intro, 2 *Hard Silence Beats*, and a 4-second closing *Title Card* within a 50–65 second final film).
2. **Dramatic Pauses & Paragraphs (Mandatory Blank Line):** In the *Copy-Ready Script*, separate every scene segment with **1 blank line (`Enter` twice)** so ElevenLabs v4 adds natural breathing pauses and FFmpeg `silencedetect` can map scene boundaries cleanly.
3. **Phonetic Pronunciation for Foreign or Invented Terms:** Split unfamiliar syllables with hyphens (e.g., `La-ter-na`, `Val-ha-lla`, `Hi-ka-ri`, `A-ra-shi`) so `Language: Auto` in Eleven v4 reads vowels accurately.
4. **Zero AI-Slop Copywriting (Mandatory):**
   - Write like a calm storyteller sitting by a tabletop lamp—concrete, visual, evocative without melodrama, guiding the viewer's eye from one paper layer or glowing window to the next.
   - **FORBIDDEN:** Never use cliché AI filler such as *"unleashing endless terror"*, *"lest the universe perish"*, *"imagine if..."*, cheap rhetorical questions (*"what could possibly happen next?"* / *"how will they survive?"*), or bloated adjectives (*"incredibly dramatic"*, *"truly extraordinary"*).

## Eleven v4 Voice Recommendations
For *Paper-cut Lightbox*, prioritize **warm, intimate, table-side voices** over booming trailer announcers:
1. **Warm & Poetic Male Storyteller (Top Choice):** `Brian`, `George`, or `Daniel` (crisp articulation, warm low end, ideal for fables, quiet history, journeys, and reflective pieces).
2. **Deep & Authoritative Male Narrator:** `Callum` or `Raunak M` (great for folklore or legends shifting from `[whispers]` to `[dramatic]`).
3. **Intimate & Expressive Female Storyteller:** `Aria` or `Charlotte` (great for lyrical tales, personal keepsakes, or heroine-centered narratives).
4. **Custom Voice Design v4:** If the user wants a bespoke timbre, craft an English voice prompt for **Try Voice Design for v4** (e.g., *"Warm, unhurried late-night storyteller at a wooden desk, gentle rasp, intimate studio mic, calm pacing"*).
